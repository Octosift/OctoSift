# Trademark policy

The Octosift code is free software under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE).
The license covers the code. It does not grant any right to the **Octosift** name, the
Octosift logo or the app icon (the "marks"). This policy explains how you may use them.

The goal is simple: when people see the name Octosift, they should get the project as its
maintainers ship it, and not be misled into thinking a different product or service comes
from or is endorsed by this project.

## You may, without asking

- **Run Octosift** for yourself, your company or your clients, unchanged, and call it Octosift.
- **Share unchanged copies**, such as the source, a Docker image or a package built from an
  official release, under the Octosift name.
- **Talk about Octosift**: write articles, tutorials, reviews, videos and comparisons, and link
  to the project.
- **Say what your work relates to**, in plain text: "based on Octosift", "a fork of Octosift",
  "works with Octosift" or "an integration for Octosift", as long as it is true and does not
  suggest that the project made or endorses it.
- **Fork the repository** to contribute changes back. A fork on GitHub that keeps the name while
  you prepare a pull request is fine.

## You may not, without written permission

- **Name a modified version Octosift.** If you change the code and give it to other people, or
  run it for them, give it a different name and remove the logo and icon. You may still say
  that it is based on Octosift.
- **Offer a hosted or managed service named Octosift**, or with a name that contains it or is
  confusingly similar to it, such as "Octosift Cloud", "Octosift Pro" or "OctoSift Hosting".
- **Use the marks in a product, company or domain name**, or in an app store listing, social
  media account or advertisement, in a way that suggests it is the official project.
- **Use the logo or icon** for anything other than referring to the official project.
- **Suggest endorsement or partnership** that does not exist.
- **Change the marks**, or combine them with your own marks.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a use is fine, or want permission for something listed above, open
an issue in this repository and describe what you have in mind. We are happy to help.

This policy may be updated from time to time. It is not legal advice and does not limit any
right you have under trademark law, such as fair use.
